AI Control Tower

One place to see, govern, and prove the value of every AI in your business.

Talk to Us
AI Control TowerServiceNow
Premier Partner · Consulting & Implementation

Know Every AI You Run

Most enterprises can't answer a simple question: how many AI tools are we actually using? Agents in ServiceNow, copilots in Microsoft 365, models your data team built, AI features inside vendor software. Each one carries risk, and few are tracked in one place.

AI Control Tower brings them together. It keeps a live inventory of every AI agent, Now Assist skill, and third-party model, and runs each one through the same governance lifecycle: intake, risk assessment, approval, and ongoing monitoring. Every AI asset has a clear owner, a steward, and a risk and compliance reviewer, so nothing goes live without the right people signing off.

Exterprise has built AI Control Tower governance flows hands-on, from intake questionnaires and impact assessments to role design, and we connect it to the AI Agents and Now Assist work we already deliver.

How AI Control Tower Works

01

Discover

Build a single inventory of AI assets across ServiceNow and third-party platforms.

02

Assess

Run intake and impact assessments that score each AI for risk before it's approved.

03

Govern

Route approvals through defined roles: AI asset owner, AI steward, and risk and compliance analyst.

04

Monitor

Track performance, usage, and value over time, and flag issues before they become incidents.
