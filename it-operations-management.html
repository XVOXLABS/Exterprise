Premier Partner · Consulting & Implementation

Evolve to the Next Stage in ITSM

IT Operations Management (ITOM) covers everything an IT department runs: applications, storage, networking, hardware and software. The goal is to keep all of it healthy on every server and node. Predictive AIOps goes a step further, spotting anomalies and flagging service issues before they cause an outage.

ITOM and ITSM: A Dynamic Duo

ITOM is about how IT runs its own systems. ITSM is about how IT delivers services to users. The two overlap more every year: an anomaly that Predictive AIOps catches in ITOM often becomes the incident that Predictive Intelligence sorts and routes in ITSM.

ServiceNow ITOM brings Event Management, Metric Intelligence and Health Log Analytics together, and it builds on the ServiceNow ITSM you already have.

We make sure your IT team can see every part of the infrastructure. Discovery and CMDB mapping keep configuration data accurate, and AI Control Tower keeps track of the AI Agents and automations that act on it.
