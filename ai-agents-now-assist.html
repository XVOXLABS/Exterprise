From Chat to Action

ServiceNow's AI Agents don't just suggest an answer — they take the next action: triaging a ticket, resolving a routine request, or escalating with full context attached. AI Agent Orchestrator coordinates multiple agents so they hand off work cleanly instead of stepping on each other, while Now Assist brings generative AI directly into the tools your teams already use — summarizing cases and drafting resolutions inside ITSM, HR Service Delivery, and Creator.

Exterprise holds direct implementation certification for Now Assist across IT Service Management, HR Service Delivery, and Creator, and designs AI Agents with the guardrails and human-in-the-loop checkpoints your business actually needs — not generic, out-of-the-box defaults.
