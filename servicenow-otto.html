ServiceNow Otto

Now Assist, Moveworks, and AI Experience, unified into one conversational front door that finishes the work — not just answers the question.

Talk to Us
ServiceNow OttoServiceNow
Premier Partner · Consulting & Implementation

Just launched: Otto was announced at ServiceNow's Knowledge 2026 event and is rolling out across the platform through the rest of the year — live first inside ServiceNow EmployeeWorks and AI Control Tower. This page reflects Otto's public launch details; Exterprise's team is tracking the rollout closely as it reaches more products.

One Front Door, Work Actually Finished

Most enterprises already have AI scattered across their tools: a copilot in the ticketing system, a chatbot on the intranet, a summarizer in the CRM. None of them talk to each other or remember the last conversation. ServiceNow Otto is ServiceNow's answer. It's a single conversational layer that brings together Moveworks, which ServiceNow acquired in 2025, with Now Assist and the AI Experience interface.

Otto doesn't replace the workflows underneath. It sits on top of the ServiceNow platform, approval chains, and permissions you already have, and AI Control Tower logs and explains every action it takes.

Exterprise already works with every piece Otto brings together. We're a Moveworks implementation partner, we hold Now Assist certifications for ITSM, HR Service Delivery, and Creator, and we've implemented AI Agent Orchestrator and AI Control Tower hands-on. That puts us in a strong position to help you prepare for Otto as it reaches more of the platform.
